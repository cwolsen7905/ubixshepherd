Shepherd is starting as {{ include "shepherd.fullname" . }}-0 in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The public listener stays closed until an owner account exists. Set one once:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it {{ include "shepherd.fullname" . }}-0 -- shepherd account set

It prints the TOTP enrolment and ten recovery codes once; keep the codes somewhere safe.
Then sign in at {{ .Values.seed.publicUrl }}, or from a laptop:

  shepherd login {{ .Values.seed.publicUrl }}

The credentials come from the Secret {{ .Values.credentials.secretName }}; see docs/kubernetes.md.
